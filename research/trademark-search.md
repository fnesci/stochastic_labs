# Trademark search: "Stochastic Labs"

*Preliminary search, October 28, 2026. Not legal advice and not a full clearance search.*

## Summary

No registered federal trademark or service mark for "Stochastic Labs" turned up in the third-party databases checked. One registered mark for the single word STOCHASTIC exists in a different field (AI software). An existing organization in Berkeley, CA has used the name "Stochastic Labs" since 2014.

## Other organizations and marks found

### Stochastic Labs (Berkeley, California)
- An incubator run by the Minerva Foundation, operating on a not-for-profit basis since 2014.
- Supports artists, engineers, scientists and entrepreneurs; past projects include Artbreeder and Earth Species Project.
- No registered trademark found, and its website shows no trademark notice.
- Even unregistered, years of use likely give it some common-law rights in its own field.
- Source: https://stochasticlabs.org/mission/

### STOCHASTIC (Stochastic Inc., Cambridge, Massachusetts)
- Serial no. 98202841, registration no. 7657981, Principal Register.
- Filed September 28, 2023; registered January 21, 2025; active.
- Class 42: AI software services (conversational AI and chatbot tools, LLM and NLP software development, related consulting).
- Games usually fall under Class 9 (downloadable games) or Class 41 (online gaming and entertainment). That's a different class, but an examiner or attorney would still consider it.
- Source: https://trademarkdir.com/trademark/stochastic-98202841

### Stochastic Labs LLP (Bengaluru, India)
- Only listed U.S. filing is a pending application for FERMI (2025), not for the company name.
- Source: https://trademarkdir.com/owner/stochastic-labs-llp

## Next steps

- Search the official USPTO database at https://tmsearch.uspto.gov for "stochastic", not just "stochastic labs". Third-party databases can lag.
- Check the California Secretary of State's state trademark registry.
- Before using the name commercially, have a trademark attorney run a clearance search.
